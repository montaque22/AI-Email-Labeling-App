# Privacy Policy for Emailable

**Last Updated:** August 4, 2026

Emailable ("we," "us," "our," or the "App") is a private, invite-only email client application. **This App is not available to the general public and is not intended for public use.** Access is granted solely at the operator's discretion. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and what you should know before using it.

The App's source code is available at [https://github.com/montaque22/AI-Email-Labeling-App](https://github.com/montaque22/AI-Email-Labeling-App) under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0). That license governs rights to the *code* — it does not make the hosted App itself a public product; see the Terms of Service for details.

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide email client functionality, the App collects and stores the following on the operator's server:

- **Account information:** Your email address, and any credentials or tokens needed to authenticate with your email provider (e.g., OAuth tokens, app passwords).
- **Email content:** Aspects of your emails necessary to display and manage your inbox, including subject lines, message bodies, sender/recipient addresses, timestamps, and attachments, as retrieved from your connected email account.
- **Integration credentials:** If you connect a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server or a third-party AI platform, the App stores the API keys, tokens, or other credentials you provide so it can make requests to those services on your behalf.
- **Technical data:** Basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, and log data, for security and debugging purposes.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

- Authenticate you and connect to your email provider
- Display, search, organize, and let you send/manage email
- Make authenticated calls to any MCP server or AI platform you've connected, using the credentials you supplied, in order to perform actions you request (e.g., summarizing, drafting, or triaging email)
- Maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the App

We do not sell your data or use your email content to train AI models.

## 3. How Your Information Is Shared

- **With services you connect:** If you link an MCP server or an AI platform, relevant email content and/or the stored credentials are sent to that service so it can perform the action you requested. That service's own privacy policy governs how it handles the data once received. We are not responsible for the practices of third-party services you choose to connect.
- **With your email provider:** To function, the App communicates with your email provider (e.g., Gmail, Outlook) using the credentials you provide.
- We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers, and we do not sell it.
- We may disclose information if required by law.

## 4. We Do Not Monitor Your Content — But Please Don't Store Sensitive Data

We do not routinely look through the database or review your stored emails, credentials, or content for sensitive information. That said, the App stores email content and API keys/tokens on the server, and no system is completely secure. **We strongly advise against using this App to store or process highly sensitive information** (e.g., financial account numbers, government IDs, medical records, passwords to unrelated systems) beyond what's inherent to normal email use. Use your own judgment about what you route through the App.

## 5. Data Loss, Availability, and Discretion

This App is operated privately and may be taken down, modified, restarted, or discontinued at any time, at the operator's sole discretion and without advance notice. **You acknowledge that using the App is at your own risk, and that you may lose access to your data — including stored emails, settings, and connected credentials — if the App is taken offline or altered.** We recommend not relying on the App as your sole record of any email or data you can't afford to lose.

## 6. Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as the App remains operational and your access continues. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. Since the App may be altered or discontinued at the operator's discretion, we cannot guarantee a specific retention period.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

You can:

- Disconnect any MCP server or AI platform integration at any time, which removes the associated stored credentials
- Request a copy or deletion of your data by contacting us at montaque.developer@gmail.com
- Revoke email account access directly through your email provider's security settings

## 8. Children's Privacy

Emailable is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. As a private, invitation-based app, access is not intended for the general public in the first place.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy at any time, at our discretion, to reflect changes in the App. The "Last Updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

## 10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to **montaque.developer@gmail.com**.
